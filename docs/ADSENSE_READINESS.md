# IWACU RWANDA — AdSense and monetisation readiness

Status reviewed: 10 November 2026. This document is an implementation and release checklist, not a promise of Google approval or future revenue.

## What is in the code

- Seven public information pages are prepared in Kinyarwanda, English and French: About, How it works, Rwanda's administrative structure, Data sources, Privacy, Contact and Terms.
- Each information page has a server-generated title and description, canonical URL, language alternates and a place in the sitemap.
- `robots.txt` is generated by Next.js. It allows public content and `/ads.txt`, and excludes admin/API routes from crawling.
- An AdSense loader and one manual responsive editorial slot are implemented behind environment-variable gates. They remain off unless the owner supplies the real publisher and slot IDs and explicitly enables them.
- The loader and ad slots are suppressed for the Kinyarwanda interface. Ad placements are limited to long-form editorial pages; the home location workflow, map, leader profiles, Ishakiro chat, notifications, emergency controls, admin area, privacy page, contact page and terms page do not receive ad slots.
- `/ads.txt` is generated only when a valid publisher ID is configured. It returns 404 until then, rather than publishing a fake publisher ID.

## Important Google language requirement

Google's current Publisher Policies supported-language list includes English and French but does not list Kinyarwanda. The application is Kinyarwanda-first. The code therefore does not load AdSense on Kinyarwanda pages.

This does **not** guarantee site approval. Google reviews the site as a whole and may decide that a site whose primary content is Kinyarwanda is not eligible. The English and French pages must be substantial, accurate and useful in their own right; machine-translated thin pages are not a safe route to approval. The owner must review the site language requirement in the AdSense account before requesting review. Do not change IWACU's Kinyarwanda-first identity or misrepresent the primary language just to obtain ads.

Official policy: https://support.google.com/publisherpolicies/answer/10436912

## Remaining blockers before applying

1. **Owner identity and contact:** the site currently does not publish a verified general contact email, phone or office address. Rwanda's DPP Law requires visitors to be informed of the controller's identity/contact details and information about the processing. The owner must provide and verify a real work contact and confirm who is the data controller. No personal email or invented address has been inserted.
2. **Privacy review:** the new privacy page documents the behaviour visible in the current code, including location processing, optional coarse location feedback, local 24-hour location snapshot, language preference, optional Web Push subscription, Ishakiro text and optional model-provider processing. The owner must confirm retention periods, provider contracts, data-subject request handling and legal wording before launch.
3. **Consent for international traffic:** before serving personalised ads to visitors in the EEA, UK or Switzerland, use a Google-certified CMP integrated with the IAB TCF. Google Privacy & Messaging or another currently certified CMP must be configured; a homemade banner is not a substitute for this requirement.
4. **Original content:** keep improving the pages with original, sourced material. Avoid empty category pages, copied government paragraphs, automated thin pages or fabricated facts. Link each factual claim to the originating public source and identify the review date.
5. **Site ownership/review:** add the site's hostname in AdSense, verify ownership with the method Google supplies, and wait until the site status is **Ready**. Do not enable ads before approval.
6. **ads.txt:** after Google supplies the publisher ID, set `ADSENSE_PUBLISHER_ID=pub-<16 digits>` or set the matching `NEXT_PUBLIC_ADSENSE_CLIENT=ca-pub-<16 digits>`. Verify `https://iwacu-rwanda-2-ahabona.vercel.app/ads.txt` returns HTTP 200 and the exact account ID.
7. **Traffic measurement:** Vercel Analytics has not been verified as enabled and no trustworthy production pageview totals were obtained in this review. After Vercel access is restored, enable Web Analytics in the project and read pageviews by date, country, device, route and referrer. Do not report guessed traffic or earnings as actual numbers.

## AdSense configuration (only after approval)

Set these Vercel environment variables for the Production environment only after the site is approved and the owner is ready:

- `NEXT_PUBLIC_ADSENSE_ENABLED=true`
- `NEXT_PUBLIC_ADSENSE_CLIENT=ca-pub-1234567890123456` (replace with the publisher ID from the AdSense account)
- `NEXT_PUBLIC_ADSENSE_SLOT_EDITORIAL=1234567890` (replace with the real responsive display-ad slot ID)
- `ADSENSE_PUBLISHER_ID=pub-1234567890123456` (same 16-digit publisher ID, with the `pub-` prefix)

The values above are format examples only. They are not real publisher IDs or slots. Never commit IDs or other account settings to source control.

Use one manually placed responsive display ad after the second section of an editorial article. Keep the ad clearly separated from content and controls. Do not use floating ads, deceptive labels, arrows, fake buttons, or placements adjacent to emergency, call, map, leader-contact or chat controls. Keep Auto ads off until the owner reviews every mobile placement.

## Traffic and break-even calculation

Use actual production data only. Once analytics are available, record monthly pageviews, ad impressions, estimated earnings and country mix from the relevant dashboards. A rough planning formula is:

`Page RPM = (Estimated ad earnings / Pageviews) × 1,000`

`Estimated monthly revenue = Pageviews × Page RPM / 1,000`

Compare actual estimated revenue with actual hosting, database, AI and domain costs. Do not assume that a given number of visitors produces a fixed amount. Do not click IWACU's own ads, ask visitors to click ads, buy paid-to-click traffic, or use traffic-exchange services. Such activity can invalidate monetisation.
